# E-Commerce Sales Analysis – Key Insights

## 1. Overall Business Performance

- Total Sales: **$2,296,195.59**
- Total Profit: **$286,241.42**
- Total Quantity Sold: **37,820**

The business generated strong overall sales and maintained a positive overall profit.

---

## 2. Category Performance

### Technology
- Highest sales: **$836,154.03**
- Highest profit: **$145,454.95**

Technology was the strongest-performing category in terms of both revenue and profitability.

### Furniture
- Sales: **$741,306.31**
- Profit: **$18,421.81**

Furniture generated high sales but comparatively low profit.

### Office Supplies
- Sales: **$718,735.24**
- Profit: **$122,364.66**

Office Supplies generated strong profitability despite having lower sales than Technology.

---

## 3. Regional Performance

- **West** generated the highest sales and profit.
- **East** was the second-best performing region.
- **Central** and **South** generated comparatively lower sales.

The West region appears to be the strongest overall market.

---

## 4. Loss-Making Sub-Categories

The following sub-categories generated negative profit:

- Tables: **-$17,725.48**
- Bookcases: **-$3,472.56**
- Supplies: **-$1,189.10**

These categories should be reviewed for pricing, discounting, and operational costs.

---

## 5. Discount Impact on Profit

Higher discount levels showed a negative impact on average profit.

Examples:

- 30% discount → Average profit: **-$45.83**
- 40% discount → Average profit: **-$111.93**
- 50% discount → Average profit: **-$310.70**

This indicates that excessive discounting can significantly reduce profitability.

---

## 6. Customer Segment Performance

- **Consumer** generated the highest sales and profit.
- **Corporate** was the second-largest segment.
- **Home Office** contributed the lowest sales among the three segments.

Consumer customers represent the largest revenue opportunity.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Review discount strategies, especially discounts above 30%.
2. Investigate loss-making sub-categories such as Tables and Bookcases.
3. Focus on profitable categories such as Technology and Office Supplies.
4. Continue strengthening sales strategies in the West and East regions.
5. Target the Consumer segment while exploring opportunities to grow Corporate and Home Office sales.
